Privacy policy

Version 2026-09-29
Haaraya is a reading programme for children. We collect only what we need to teach reading and show parents progress. We do not show ads, sell data, or use third-party tracking. Parents can download or delete their child's data at any time from the Parent Dashboard.

Who we are

Haaraya Education ("Haaraya", "we") runs this website and is responsible for the data it holds. Contact us about anything in this policy at info@haarayaeducation.org.

What we collect

AboutWhatWhy
Parent or schoolName, email, password (stored encrypted), country, optional phone, planTo run the account, sign you in and recover it
ChildFirst and last name, passport display name, year of birth, reading confidence, avatar choicesTo create the Reading Passport and start at the right level
Child's readingBooks opened and finished, pages reached, stamps, reading-check answers and scores, saved books, Odyssey log entriesTo choose the next book, award stamps and show progress to the parent or teacher
DevicesA random device ID, browser type, IP address and time of each sign-inTo stop one account being shared beyond a family, and to protect accounts
ErrorsTechnical error reports (page, browser, what failed), with names and typed text removedTo find and fix problems quickly

We do not ask children for their email, phone, address, photo, or location, and there is no chat or public sharing between users.

Parental consent

A parent or legal guardian must create the account and give consent before we collect any information about a child. Schools that enrol pupils confirm they have authority to do so for teaching purposes. We record the date and version of the policy each person agreed to. If we learn we have a child's information without consent, we delete it.

Who can see it

  • You: parents see their own children; teachers and school admins see pupils in their school.
  • Haaraya staff: only to support you or keep the service running.
  • Service providers who process data on our behalf and may not use it for anything else: Supabase (database and sign-in), GitHub Pages (website hosting), Sentry (error reports) and our email provider (account and progress emails).

We never sell or rent personal data, and we never give it to advertisers. We disclose data only if the law requires it.

Emails

We send account emails (confirmation, password reset) and, to parents, a weekly progress summary. Every summary has a one-click unsubscribe link.

How long we keep it

  • Account and reading data: while the account is open. When you delete a child or your account it is erased from the live database straight away and from backups within 30 days.
  • Sign-in records: 12 months.
  • Payment records: as long as tax law requires.

Your rights

Under the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023 and, for US families, the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA), you can:

  • See and download your child's data: Parent Dashboard → Privacy & your data → Download data.
  • Correct it: Parent Dashboard → Edit details.
  • Delete a child or your whole account: Parent Dashboard → Privacy & your data.
  • Withdraw consent and stop further collection, by deleting the child or emailing us.
  • Complain to the Nigeria Data Protection Commission (ndpc.gov.ng) or, in the US, the Federal Trade Commission (ftc.gov).

Or just email info@haarayaeducation.org. We reply within 30 days.

Security

Data is sent over encrypted connections and stored in a database where each family can reach only its own records. Passwords are never stored in readable form.

Changes

If we change what we collect or how we use a child's data, we will email parents and ask for fresh consent before the change applies to their child.
